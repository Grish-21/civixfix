About CivicFix

A centralized municipal grievance redressal platform engineered to bridge the communication gap between citizens and municipal administrations.

Engineering Idea Lab Project

Urban System Problem Statement

"To observe operational patterns in urban environments and demonstrate a very basic system for improvement."

CivicFix is developed as a prototype to tackle urban deterioration, lack of transparency in civic administration, and delayed public infrastructure maintenance. Through structured ticketing, real-time status transitions, and closed-loop citizen feedback, the system optimizes urban operations.

Urban Lifecycle Redefined
Observation • Routing • Resolution • Verification

Core Objectives

1. Simplified Reporting

Effortless multi-category complaint logging with visual evidence and locality detection.

2. Unique Public Tracking

Generate tamper-proof unique tracking identifiers (CF-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) for transparent auditing.

3. Department Delegation

Automatic and manual routing of grievances to dedicated municipal wings (Roads, Sanitation, Water, Electrical).

4. Audit & Accountability

Preserve timestamped audit logs for every state transition, preventing negligence or unverified closures.

5. Evidence-Based Proof

Require resolution notes and optional photographic proof prior to marking tasks resolved.

6. Citizen Evaluation

Empower citizens to rate municipal service quality (1-5 stars) and record civic satisfaction metrics.

Planned Architectural Expansions (Future Scope)

The core database and route interfaces are architected to cleanly accommodate the following phase-two capabilities:

GIS / Map Integration & GPS Tagging: Interactive geo-coordinate pinning on OpenStreetMap or Google Maps.
AI Automated Classification & Duplicate Detection: Vision-based validation to cluster co-located duplicate reports.
Automated SMS & Email Dispatcher: Instant SMS/email notifications on status milestones.
Native Mobile Application: Cross-platform Flutter/React Native application using REST API wrappers.
