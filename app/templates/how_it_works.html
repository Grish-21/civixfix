How CivicFix Operates

A complete look at the end-to-end municipal grievance resolution lifecycle from citizen reporting to administrative verification and closure.

Stage 1
1. Citizen Registration

Citizens register with their verified mobile number and residential locality, providing a credible point of contact.

Stage 2
2. Complaint Lodging

Submit issue details, choose the category, pinpoint landmark/locality, and upload photo evidence.

Stage 3
3. Unique ID Allocation

A unique reference code (CF-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) is generated for instant public tracking and auditing.

Stage 4
4. Administrative Review

Municipal admins review veracity. If valid, it is marked Verified; if spam or out-of-jurisdiction, it is Rejected with logged remarks.

Stage 5
5. Routing & Field Execution

Complaint is routed to the designated wing (e.g. Road Dept, Water Dept) with priority rating (Low/Medium/High/Urgent) and set to In Progress.

Stage 6
6. Resolution & Feedback

Field evidence and resolution notes are uploaded. Once marked Resolved, the citizen rates the service (1-5 stars) and leaves remarks.

The Formal Status Progression

Submitted Under Review Verified Assigned In Progress Resolved

Every single update writes an immutable entry into the complaint_status_history table, capturing the previous status, new status, responsible administrator, and timestamp.
